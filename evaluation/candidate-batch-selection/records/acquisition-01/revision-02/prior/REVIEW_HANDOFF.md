## Fixed decisions

- Eight methods: RF mean, GP mean, unchanged original policy, random, qPO latent/observation, and pTop10 latent/observation. Batch sizes 5/10/20, seeds 0–4, 8,192 joint draws give **3,600 planned selection rows**. Each pool remains intact; equality groups share latent samples but retain every candidate ID.
- Primary: pTop10 latent versus GP mean and the original policy at batch 10, using the original ID-resolved measured-top10 recall. Observation-distribution methods and fractional-gold-tie metrics are prespecified sensitivities, never alternatives chosen after results. qPO is assessed for best-candidate capture, with recall, selected mean/best activity, and regret also reported.
- The [qPO paper, including Supplement S1](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.06333v2), distinguishes unique-winner additive optimization from nonunique maxima. Our independent qPO implementation greedily adds the candidate covering the most still-uncovered sampled winner events. This agrees with additive fixed-b qPO when winners are unique; otherwise it is the declared S1 greedy coverage approximation, not a global optimum guarantee. pTop10 uses expected membership with fractional allocation at exact sampled boundary ties. Its fixed-b additive utility is separate from qPO's purpose.
- Equal latent variables are identified jointly from mean and covariance, using dimension-scaled float64 roundoff tolerances (`64*eps*n*scale`) and complete-link groups. Near-but-distinct candidates remain separate. Symmetrization, spectral roundoff removal, group identities, tolerances, and maximum numerical correction are recorded. There is no artificial diagonal jitter. Observation samples add only the saved likelihood variance to the same latent draws, with a separate RNG stream.
- Original ranking and gold top10 ties use `(-value, candidate_id)`. Posterior acquisition-score ties use GP mean then candidate ID. Fractional gold sensitivity uniformly allocates the available boundary slots, with separate ID-best, any-best, and fractional-best capture fields.
- Average seeds within each task before task-macro reporting. Paired intervals resample target clusters, keeping the two CHEMBL237 endpoints together. Raw paired analysis requires all five seeds for a task. Partial successes, full-denominator operational zero-hit failures, and GP-mean fallback policies remain separately visible; selection failure is not a measured biological negative.
- One task worker at a time, two CPU threads, 180-second wall limit, 180/181 CPU-second soft/hard limit, 2 GiB address-space limit, no retry. Baselines are saved before posterior work. If the worker fails or times out, missing planned method/seed/batch rows become explicit failures. Posterior-method GP-mean fallback IDs are separate fields. Timing and peak memory are recorded when the worker exits normally or catches an error; an OS-killed worker may lack its internal resource receipt.

## Implemented files and boundaries

`prepare_protocol.py` verifies the original frozen protocol/source pins, every prediction seal, all reconstruction receipts/NPZ hashes, full identities and label-free feature membership, finiteness, and saved GP marginal agreement. It then writes label-free input packets and the draft. Gold paths and expected hashes are copied as metadata; the gold files are not opened.

`acquisition.py` is the independent numerical implementation. `selection_worker.py` denies evaluation-label reads and network access, and enforces process resource limits. `run_selection.py` fills the full 120-row ledger for every task and seals all 30 terminal receipts and output artifacts.

`score_selection.py` first verifies all source/input pins and **every task's seal, receipt, artifact hash, row identity, count, uniqueness, candidate membership, batch size, and baseline finiteness**. Only after that complete gate does it hash/read any gold. It then checks gold identity and finiteness, computes both fixed tie metrics, and emits raw, operational, and fallback summaries with paired denominators. Synthetic late-task corruption tests prove that neither the gold hash reader nor gold parser is reached early.

`freeze_protocol.py` is prepared but not executed. After root review, run it with a concrete `--root-review-reference`; then `run_selection.py`, and only after its complete seal, `score_selection.py`. Use `../../gp-env/bin/python -B` from this directory with `LD_LIBRARY_PATH=@PRIVATE_HOST_PATH and two BLAS threads. A technical repair after freeze requires a new preserved attempt, not overwriting this run.
