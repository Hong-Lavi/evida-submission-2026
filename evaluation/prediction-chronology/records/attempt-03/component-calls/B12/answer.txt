The next useful scientific step is to specify a genuinely new measurement instance and freeze its conditional expectations before its outcome becomes available. A focused proposed test would compare the drug-versus-vehicle response under control conditions, verified MKP1 loss, and functional rescue, with matched TGFβ1 exposure, PGE2 background, viability controls, and independent donors. Define the endpoint, effect direction, timing, and meaningful attenuation or retention thresholds in advance. Attenuation with effective loss and restoration with rescue would strengthen an MKP1-dependent contribution; retention despite verified loss would support an MKP1-independent component under those conditions. Partial responses would retain coexistence as an explanation. This is a proposed check; no new experiment was performed here.
