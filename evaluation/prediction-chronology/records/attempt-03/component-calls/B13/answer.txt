For p1-assigned, the downstream endpoint, perturbation direction and quantitative decision thresholds are not yet specified. The following expectations assume effective MKP1 loss of function, adequate assay sensitivity, preserved viability and stimulation competence, and an interpretable functional rescue. Define the response as the drug-versus-matched-vehicle effect within each perturbation condition. If p-p38/total p38 is chosen, Outcome 0—response lost or lower—means reduced drug-induced suppression, not necessarily lower raw phosphorylation. Outcome 1 means that suppression is retained. This operational definition is proposed here, not an existing recorded measurement specification.

- Under hA, effective MKP1 loss should attenuate the drug-associated downstream effect if MKP1 contributes detectably in the chosen conditions. Functional rescue should restore that component. Complete loss is not required by a contribution hypothesis: residual response can reflect coexisting pathways. Attenuation with specific rescue would strengthen hA, while leaving hB possible. Retention despite verified, sustained perturbation and a sufficiently precise assay would weaken a substantial MKP1-dependent contribution at that endpoint and time; it would not exclude contributions in other conditions or masked by compensation.
- Under hB, a sufficiently MKP1-independent contribution can preserve the drug response despite MKP1 loss. In that conditional case, restoring MKP1 is not required to recover the retained component. However, hB explicitly permits coexistence, so partial attenuation and rescue are also compatible with it. Outcome 0 alone therefore cannot reject hB, and Outcome 1 cannot establish a particular alternative pathway. The broad hypotheses do not justify mutually exclusive predictions, numerical probabilities or likelihood ratios from this packet.
